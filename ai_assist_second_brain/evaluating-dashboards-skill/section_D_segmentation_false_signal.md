# Segmentation & False Signal Risk

Ensure aggregate trends do not conceal divergent subgroup performance.

## Principle

Aggregate metrics are summaries. Decisions are made on subgroups.

---

## Detailed Checks

### 1. Mandatory Segmentation Review

Evaluate segmentation by:
- Channel (paid vs organic)
- Device (mobile vs desktop)
- Geography
- Plan tier
- Cohort (signup week/month)

Red flags:
- Conversion stable overall but mobile declining.
- Retention stable but enterprise churn rising.
- Satisfaction high overall but low for new users.

---

### 2. Simpson’s Paradox Risk

Ask:
- Could subgroup trends reverse the aggregate?
- Are acquisition mix changes distorting outcomes?

Red flags:
- New user growth masking poor returning-user retention.
- Channel shift inflating top-line metrics.

---

## Expanded Example Failures

- Company-wide NPS hiding dissatisfaction among high-value segment.
- Cohort retention chart absent from subscription business dashboard.
- Filter exists but defaults obscure important segments.
