# Diagnostic Power

Measure whether users can quickly identify drivers of change.

## Principle

Dashboards should shorten investigation cycles.

---

## Detailed Checks

### 1. Cause Proximity

Primary KPI should have:
- Supporting breakdown directly below.
- Driver metrics nearby.
- Clear visual hierarchy of outcome → drivers.

Red flags:
- KPI tile isolated at top.
- Drivers on separate page.
- Requires separate analytics tool to investigate.

---

### 2. Click Depth

Count interactions needed to diagnose.

Red flags:
- >2 clicks to see driver breakdown.
- Hidden filters required.
- Separate tabs for related metrics.

---

## Expanded Example Failures

- Revenue drop shown but no funnel conversion chart.
- Activation rate displayed without onboarding step breakdown.
- Retention decline without churn reason categorization.
